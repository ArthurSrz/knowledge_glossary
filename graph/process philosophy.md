**[[Process]] philosophy** (also **[[ontology]] of becoming** or **processism**) is an approach in philosophie that identifies processes, changes, or shifting relationships as the only real experience of everyday living  In opposition to the classical view of change as illusory (as argued by Parmenides) or accidental (as argued by Aristotle), [[process]] philosophy posits transient occasions of change or _becoming_ as the most fundamental things of the ordinary everyday real world.
